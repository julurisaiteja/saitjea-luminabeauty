# Lumina Beauty — Studio Strategy

1. **Unique position:** Ritual-first beauty with shade science — champagne/mineral palette, not purple-pink AI spa gradients.
2. **Customer:** Everyday glow seekers who want undertone clarity, AM/PM structure, and ingredient transparency on PDPs.
3. **5-second feel:** Soft golden light, confident and gentle — skin that catches light without clinical coldness.
4. **Signature:** Shade Wheel on `/shade` and homepage — conic undertone ring + depth/tone controls + AM/PM ritual timeline.
5. **Journey:** Film hero → Shade Wheel → vanity kit cards → deep PDP (ingredients, variants, FAQ) → cart → GLOWKIT checkout → success.
6. **Conversion hooks:** Offer banner, kit upsell, ritual reviews, newsletter, AI assistant with routine/shade answers, sticky shop CTA.
7. **Motion & a11y:** Shimmer headline, pulse glow accents, marquee; reduced-motion disables decorative animation.
8. **Tech:** Next.js 15.5.7 App Router, Tailwind 3, Cormorant + Figtree typography, localStorage cart, mock Stripe flow.
